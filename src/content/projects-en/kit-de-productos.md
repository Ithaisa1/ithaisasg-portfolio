The other products are only shown as images, because they are the files being sold. The demos are in Spanish.

## The five products

![Advert for the "Flexbox & Grid without the pain" guide, with sample pages from the PDF.](../../assets/proyectos/kit/01-guia-flexbox-grid.jpg)

**"Flexbox & Grid without the pain" guide.** A 17-page PDF with more than 60 visual examples and 8 layout recipes to copy.

![Advert for the digital wedding invitation, shown on a phone.](../../assets/proyectos/kit/02-invitacion-boda.jpg)

**Digital wedding invitation.** A website that opens on the phone with a countdown, a map and RSVP via WhatsApp.

![Advert for the digital business card, with a photographer's profile and contact buttons.](../../assets/proyectos/kit/03-tarjeta-digital.jpg)

**Digital business card.** A personal page with contact details, links and services, edited from a single block of data.

![Advert for the pack of 49 UI components, on mobile and desktop.](../../assets/proyectos/kit/04-pack-componentes-ui.jpg)

**Pack of 49 UI components.** Buttons, cards, forms, loaders and more, in HTML and CSS with no libraries, with a search box and a button to copy the code. The colours of the whole pack change with a single variable.

![Advert for the restaurant website, on mobile and desktop.](../../assets/proyectos/kit/05-landing-restaurante.jpg)

**Restaurant website.** A complete one-page site for a hospitality business, designed so the owner can customise it by following step-by-step instructions.

## Decisions

- **No dependencies:** each product works by opening an HTML file. Buyers do not need to install anything.
- **Built to be customised:** the texts that need changing are marked in the code, and each product includes step-by-step instructions.
- **For two audiences:** templates for businesses and individuals, and learning resources and components for students and developers.

## My contribution

I chose which products to create and for whom, defined the design of each one and prepared the sales approach. The code and the texts were made with AI assistance, and I led and reviewed the work.
